The Visionary

Meet Our Founder

"I started this journey not to build a business, but to preserve a tradition — the pure, honest flavours of our ancestors."

With over two decades of experience in traditional Sri Lankan spice cultivation, our founder established Thalgampala Thunapaha with a single mission: to bring the finest Ceylon spices to wholesale and retail customers. Beginning from a small home kitchen in Thalgampala, the brand has grown to serve over 25 branches across the Southern Province and beyond.

His deep knowledge of Ayurvedic herbs and natural farming practices ensures that every product carries the true essence of Ceylon spice culture — uncompromising in quality, authentic in taste, and honest in origin. Each batch is personally overseen to ensure the highest standards.
